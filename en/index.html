Services

Your sponsorship deserves more than a logo

Visibility is the starting point, never the final deliverable. Behind every partnership, SportsMatters makes sure everything is in order, from the contract down to the last VIP table.

Access to the organisers

RBFA, ASO, Flanders Classics: we know their partnership offers inside out. We guide your brand to the right one and make sure the contract is honoured by every party.

Negotiated visibility

Stand placement, locations, branding assets: we secure and verify on site what you were promised. Contractual visibility becomes actual visibility.

Field logistics

Set-up, flows, timing, contingencies: the operation runs without your teams having to think about it. You arrive, everything is ready.

Hospitality & VIP tables

Guest welcome, VIP tables, a seamless journey from car park to podium: your clients experience the event as privileged guests, in your colours.

Goodies & entertainment

Measurement & phygital

About

Sport, since 1995

Philippe Verbert has spent more than 25 years in the field of Belgian sport: events, sponsorship, brand activation. Co-founder of Sportscom, he launched SportsMatters to give brands senior, direct guidance with no unnecessary layers.

A deliberately lean structure: you talk to the person who designs the concept and stands in the field. His playground: federations and event organisers, RBFA, ASO, Flanders Classics and many more, whose offers and backstages he knows inside out. Between brand and organiser, he is the trusted link who guarantees the promised visibility and an honoured contract, watching over every detail: logistics, stand placement, goodies, guest welcome, VIP tables.
